Readings

  • Dickinson, M., C. Brew, and D. Meurers. 2012. Language and Computers. Wiley-Blackwell.
  • Johnson, K. 2008. Quantitative Methods in Linguistics. Blackwell Publishing.
  • Jurafsky, D. and J.H. Martin. 2009. Speech and Language Processing. 3rd edition. Currently only available online.
  • Lutz, M. and D. Ascher. 2004. Learning Python. 2nd edition. O'Reilly.
  • Raschka, S. 2025. Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch) Manning.
  • Tunstall, L., L. von Werra, and T. Wolf. 2022. Natural Language Processing with Transformers: Building Language Applications with Hugging Face. Revised edition. O'Reilly.
